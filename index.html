Research Overview

My research explores the spatial, social, and design cognition of humans and AI in built environments and social landscapes. Drawing on HCI and cognitive computational neuroscience — neuroimaging, eye-tracking, and machine learning — I develop evidence-based computational design tools and examine how conversational AI measures and reshapes psychological distance at scale. In parallel, I pursue fundamental questions in ML/DL through a NeuroAI lens: shortcut learning, cognitive map formation, and biologically plausible learning mechanisms.
