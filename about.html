Hi, I'm YC — a graduate student (from 02/2025) at National Tsing Hua University (NTHU), working with Dr. Po-Chih Kuo in the HMI Lab, Department of Computer Science.

My research explores the spatial, social, and design cognition of humans and AI in built environments, combining human-computer interaction (HCI) with cognitive computational neuroscience — integrating cognitive science, neuroimaging, and machine learning to study how people experience and shape their environments.

I collaborate with Dr. Yi-Chieh (EJ) Lee at the AI4SG Lab, NUS on conversational AI and information-seeking behavior, and was previously trained in fMRI under Dr. Chih-Mao Huang at NCTU/HKU.
